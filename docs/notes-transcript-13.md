## Live quiz user testing (most actionable)

Thea walked Gabi through a series of dosha assessment questions in real time, watching how she responded. This is field research for roadmap item 18.

**Questions tested and reactions:**

**Body frame**
- Options: Small and narrow / Medium, athletic frame / Large, broad frame
- Gabi: answered clearly (small/narrow)
- Works as-is

**Wrist circumference test**
- Instruction: wrap thumb and middle finger around your wrist — do they overlap, connect, or not connect?
- Tactile and fun. Gabi engaged with it. Strong question — keep this.

**Weight patterns**
- Options: Gain weight easily / Lose weight easily / Pretty moderate and consistent / Struggle to gain weight
- Gabi: moderate and consistent
- Works as single-select

**Hair quality**
- Options: Dry, coarse, kind of curly / Fine and straight / Thick, full, luxurious
- Gabi: neither — "a medium of thick and luxurious... not fine, but not super thick. Oily but also dry."
- Problem: no combination option. Thea acknowledged in the moment: "maybe in the app those are like... and maybe I need to do like click all that apply."
- Action: multi-select or "combination" option needed

**Skin quality**
- Options: Dry, rough, sensitive, red, thick, soft, moist
- Gabi: sensitive, red, dry — multiple applied
- Thea: "I would do check all that apply"
- Action: multi-select required

**Eye quality** *(transcript cuts off mid-question)*
- Options started: small/active/dark...
- Incomplete — follow up with Thea for the full eye question

**Key UX takeaways:**
- Every question needs a "none of these" / "other" escape
- Skin and hair need "check all that apply" — single-select produces wrong results for combination types
- The wrist test is a high-engagement tactile question — worth keeping

## App experience preferences

**Quiet app.** Thea wants L. Glow to be a quiet app — no auto-playing sound. She compared it unfavorably to TikTok (loud, you know going in) vs. original Instagram (just photos, no sound). "I would want the choice" on audio — opt-in, never forced.

**Voice input interest.** Thea herself would prefer to speak answers to check-in questions rather than type them. But she recognized people are often around others and wouldn't want their voice heard. Voice input = option, never the only path.
