## Section structure

### Physical structure (Prakriti — constitutional physical traits)

Each characteristic has three options (Vata / Pitta / Kapha). Single-select is appropriate here — these are relatively fixed traits.

| Characteristic | Vata | Pitta | Kapha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body frame | Thin (naturally, without outside influences) | Medium, muscular | Stocky, stout |
| Bone structure | Light, narrow bones; joints prominent | Moderate, medium build | Thick and heavy |
| Body weight | Light and variable | Moderate and muscular | Can be overweight, heavy |
| Complexion / skin | Dry, gray, rough, cool, thin | Rosy, oily, moderate thickness, ruddy | Pale, light-skinned, moist, cool, thick |
| Hair | Dry, coarse, curly, brittle | Fine, light in color, oily; grays early; baldness | Thick, oily, luxurious, wavy |
| Teeth | Irregular and crooked (before orthodontics) | Moderate, yellowish | Large, strong, white, healthy |
| Eyes | Small | Medium, deep-set, sharp; typically blue or green | Large, luxurious lashes |
| Nose | Small and narrow | Medium | Large, wide bridge |
| Lips | Thin, small, often chapped | Medium | Large, thick |
| Chin | Thin and angular | Tapered and angular | Doubled and round |
| Neck | Long and thin | Medium | Short and thick |
| Fingers / palms | Thin, long, narrow | Medium and/or square | Thick, fleshy, short |
| Face | Oval or angular (thinner) | Angular | Round |

**Wrist circumference test (Thea added this):**
Wrap your middle finger and thumb around the opposite wrist — do they overlap, touch, or is there a gap? Good tactile proxy for bone structure. Confirmed across both transcript 13 and 14 as a strong question.

**Face shape addition (Thea's suggestion):**
Show reference images of face shapes to help users identify their own. Also offer a photo upload option (clearly optional, "if they're comfortable") to help with the analysis.

## UX requirements

**Multi-select for physical function and psychological function:**
> "I want these to be like a quick all. Click all that apply because sometimes you don't necessarily just live in one area."

This applies especially to the psychological section — fear, anger, and attachment can all coexist. Single-select produces wrong results here.

Physical structure (fixed traits like body frame, bone structure) can stay single-select.

**"None of these" / skip escape on every question.** She explicitly called this out.
