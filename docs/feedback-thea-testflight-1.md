After completing the dosha quiz and landing in the recommendations/day view, Thea had no way to get back to the home screen. Tapping the L.Glow logo did nothing. The bottom nav was not accessible. She had to force-close the app and relaunch. This is a hard blocker for any new user.

Tapping Reminders from the hamburger drawer led to a screen she couldn't navigate away from. Had to force-close the app. Needs investigation.

**Tapping past journal entries doesn't open the full entry.**
In the Earlier section of the journal screen, entries show an excerpt. Tapping them does nothing — no full-entry view. She noticed the entry was cut off and couldn't read it.

**Practices and rituals taps do nothing.**

## Quiz and check-in

**Q3 "How is your digestion?" needs reframing.**
The question triggered "strong and sharp, I get irritable if meals are skipped" as the matching answer. Her feedback: regular people associate "digestion" with elimination, not hunger or agni. Suggested reframe: "How is your hunger?" with "digestion" in smaller text alongside it, or rework the question entirely. This needs Thea's input on exact wording — do not change without her.

**Q4 sleep options are incomplete.**
She sleeps 8–9 hours soundly but none of the options fit. "Sound and short" doesn't make sense — she's sound but definitely not short. Couldn't honestly select any option. Wants an "Other" option added as a fallback. Broader issue: the sleep options need a review pass with Thea before changing.

**Quiz result was inaccurate for her.**
Answered as herself, got Vata. She's Vata-Pitta / Pitta-Vata (roughly equal split). The quiz isn't capturing the dual-dosha pattern accurately. Her take: 10 questions may not be enough to reliably identify prakriti, but she did like that it was short. Needs a deeper rework with her — flagged in roadmap as item 18.

**100% single-dosha results are wrong.**
When she answered as a friend she knows to be strongly Kapha, the result came back 100% Kapha. Her words: "Nothing is 100% of one. There's always gonna be a little bit of another." The scoring algorithm needs to ensure all three doshas always have at least some representation in the result. Even a strong Kapha will be something like 80/12/8.

## Copy and content

**"Welcome back, Vata" — change to user's name.**
Greeting users by their dosha feels impersonal and clinically off. She wants "Welcome back, [name]." This requires collecting the user's name somewhere (intake form has it; may need a simpler name-capture on first launch in the interim).

**Lifestyle notes are overwhelming.**
The recommendations text is dense and long. Her read: a regular user will see a wall of text and shut down. Wants it broken into 1–2 bullet points max, or formatted so it's scannable rather than a paragraph. This is a layout/presentation issue, not a content rewrite — but she'll want to be involved in the final format since it's her voice.

**"Just for Today" needs a call to action.**
Three suggestions are shown. She chose one but wasn't sure a regular user would know they're only supposed to pick one. Wants a simple instruction — "choose one" or a visual affordance that makes it clear. Positive note: she likes the section overall.

**Learn section overuses her name.**
Seeing "Thea's voice" and similar references throughout the Learn section didn't sit well with her. Her framing: she wants it to feel like Ayurveda, not like her personal brand. "My practices aren't really going to be for everybody." The language should center the practice, with Thea as the guide — not Thea as the subject. Needs a copy pass once content is populated, not a structural change.

**About Thea bio needs a full rewrite — she'll do it.**
Current bio is placeholder/draft and she knows it. She specifically wants to add her personal history with disordered eating growing up in the 90s — her words: "that part of my journey is really important to include, and I think it's going to resonate with the audience I have." She was careful to note the distinction: she can talk about disordered eating (her lane), but not clinical eating disorders (not her lane). This is her content to write; flag it as waiting on her.

**Credentials formatting under her name.**
Current stacking is wrong. She wants: Certified Wellness Coach → Ayurveda Medicine → RYT (each on its own line, in that order). RYT can stay or move below — her call.

## UX / design

**No search in the herbs section.**
Wanted to look up ginger specifically. Had to scroll through the full list. Her ask: a simple search/filter input so a user can type a herb name and jump directly to it. Loves the warm/cooling/heating tags — just wants faster access.

**Practices / rituals numbers are confusing.**
On the You tab: "Day 12," "84 practices," "0 rituals," "78% of your rituals kept" — she didn't understand what any of these meant or what she was supposed to be doing to earn rituals. The numbers aren't explained and the taps don't do anything. Needs either a cleaner explanation or a rethink of what this section is tracking and communicating.

**Saved favorites — can't find how to save things.**
There's a Saved Favorites section visible on the You tab but she couldn't figure out where the save/bookmark action lives anywhere in the app. If it exists, it's not discoverable. If it doesn't exist yet, the section shouldn't be visible.
